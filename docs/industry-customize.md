# 🎨 Industry Customization Guide

You can easily adjust the parameters in `event-generators/shared/config.yaml` to tailor the numbers to a specific enterprise prospect.

### Available Tuning Levers:
- `stores_count` / `towers_count` / `facilities_count` — scale up or down based on client size.
- `regions` — change naming to match client's actual geographical operating zones.
- `anomaly_rate` — increase frequency during quick 5-minute elevator pitches to ensure immediate alerts trigger on screen.
