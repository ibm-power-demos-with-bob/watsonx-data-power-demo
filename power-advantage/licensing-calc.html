Why Does This Math Work?

Under IBM Container Licensing rules (Passport Advantage / IBM License Service), 1 x86 hardware thread equals 1 Virtual Processor Core (VPC). On IBM Power, with SMT4 active (4 hardware threads per physical core), vCPUs map to VPCs with a divisor of 4 (vCPU ÷ 4 = VPC). Because watsonx.data is licensed in Resource Units (RU) based on VPCs, customer software license requirements are slashed dramatically.
